Our Story

Built on trust, driven by passion

Ng'ang'a Cars! was founded with a simple belief: buying a car in Kenya should feel exciting, transparent, and effortless. We curate every vehicle that enters our lot, verifying logbooks, service histories, and mechanical condition before it ever reaches our showroom.

Our digital-first approach means you can browse, compare, and even book a test drive from your phone. No pushy salespeople, no hidden fees. Just real cars, real prices, and a real commitment to getting you behind the wheel of something you'll love.

Every vehicle in our inventory is hand-picked. We focus on quality over volume, and we'd rather turn away a car than sell you something we wouldn't drive ourselves.

James Ng'ang'a, Founder
